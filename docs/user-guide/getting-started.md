# Getting started

## Roles

Everyone who signs in to Domar has exactly one role. It decides what they see.

| Role | Who | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Supervisor** | Runs the operation | Plans and assigns jobs, manages properties, bookings and the team, reviews finished work |
| **Worker** | Cleaners, handymen, electricians | Sees the jobs assigned to them, works through checklists, takes photos, finishes jobs |
| **Owner** | Owns one or more properties | Follows the work done on their own properties and talks to the team |

## Accepting an invite

Domar has no public sign-up. A supervisor invites you:

1. You receive an invite link from your supervisor (by message or email).
2. Open it and enter your name and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Select **Create account**. You are signed in and taken to your start page.

Invite links work **once** and expire after **7 days**. If yours says
*"This invite link is invalid or has expired"*, ask your supervisor for a new one.

## Signing in

Go to your Domar address (your supervisor will tell you what it is) and sign
in with your email and password. Domar remembers you on that device for
up to 14 days, so you don't need to sign in every time.

Depending on your role, you land on:

- **Workers:** *My Day*
- **Supervisors:** the *Dashboard*
- **Owners:** *My properties*

## Install it on your phone

Domar works in any modern browser, and you can add it to your home screen so
it opens like an app:

- **iPhone (Safari):** Share button → *Add to Home Screen*
- **Android (Chrome):** menu ⋮ → *Install app* or *Add to Home screen*

## Times and dates

All times are shown in Central European time (Europe/Zagreb), wherever you
are when you open the app.

## Forgot your password?

There is no self-service password reset yet. Ask your supervisor; they can
send you a fresh invite.
