# Getting started

## Roles

Everyone who signs in to Domar has exactly one role. It decides what they see.

| Role | Who | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Supervisor** | Runs the operation | Plans, prices and assigns jobs; manages properties, bookings and the team; reviews finished work; bills owners |
| **Worker** | Cleaners, handymen, electricians, gardeners | Sees their assigned jobs, bids on open jobs, works through checklists, takes photos, finishes jobs |
| **Owner** | Owns one or more properties | Follows the work on their properties, comments, rates the work, downloads bills |

Each property-care company has its own separate space in Domar; you only ever
see your own company's people and properties. Whoever runs the Domar
installation is a **platform admin**. They set up companies but don't take
part in day-to-day work (see [Self-hosting](../self-hosting.md)).

## Accepting an invite

Domar has no public sign-up. A supervisor invites you:

1. You receive an invite link from your supervisor (by message or email).
2. Open it, then enter your name and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Select **Create account**, then sign in with your email and new password.

Invite links work **once** and expire after **7 days**. If yours says
*"This invite link is invalid or has expired"*, ask your supervisor for a new one.

## Signing in

Go to your Domar address (your supervisor will tell you what it is) and sign
in with your email and password. Capitalisation in the email doesn't matter.
Domar remembers you on that device for up to 14 days.

Depending on your role, you land on:

- **Workers:** *My Day*
- **Supervisors:** the *Agenda* dashboard
- **Owners:** *My properties*

## Your profile

Select your initials in the top-right corner to open your profile, where you
can upload a profile photo. Your name and email are managed by your supervisor.

## On your phone

Domar works in any modern browser, and you can add it to your home screen so
it opens like an app:

- **iPhone (Safari):** Share button → *Add to Home Screen*
- **Android (Chrome):** menu ⋮ → *Install app* or *Add to Home screen*

Workers can also use the **Domar Worker** Android app, which works offline. See
the [guide for workers](workers.md#the-app).

## Times and dates

All times are shown in Central European time (Europe/Zagreb), wherever you
are when you open the app.

## Forgot your password?

There's no self-service password reset yet. Ask your supervisor, who will
pass it on to whoever runs your Domar installation.
