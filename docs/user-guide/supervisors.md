# Guide for supervisors

As a supervisor you plan the work, keep the team busy and decide when a job
is really done.

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows the agenda of active jobs, grouped by day. At the top, a
**needs attention** strip lists jobs that can't move on without you:

- **Drafts**, which have no worker assigned yet
- **Declined** jobs, with the worker's reason

## Creating a job

**New job** → pick the property, the type (cleaning, repair, electrical,
plumbing, inspection, other), a title, an optional description, and the date
and time window.

When you choose a worker, Domar helps you:

- **Skill filter:** only workers qualified for the job type are offered.
- **Conflict hint:** if that worker already has a job in the same time window,
  you'll see which one. This is a warning only; you can still assign.

Choose **Leave unassigned** to save the job as a draft and assign it later.

If the property has a cleaning checklist template, it is copied onto the job
when the job is created. Later edits to the template don't change existing jobs.

## Bookings and turnover cleanings

Under **Bookings**, enter guest stays: the guest checkout time, and if known,
the next check-in.

For each checkout Domar suggests a turnover cleaning, by default a three-hour
window from checkout. If the next guest arrives sooner, the window is
shortened and the booking is marked as a **tight gap**, so you can plan
extra hands. **Create turnover job** opens the new-job form already filled in
with the property and time window; pick a worker and save. Once created, the
booking links to its job.

## Reviewing finished work

**To verify** lists every job a worker has marked done. Open one to review the
checklist, photos and the worker's notes, then:

- **Verify work** to approve it. The job is complete.
- **Reopen** to send it back to the worker. You must say what needs redoing; your
  reason is added to the job as an internal comment.

See [How a job moves through Domar](job-lifecycle.md) for every status.

## Comments: internal vs. shared

When you write a comment, the **Visible to owner** switch decides who can read it:

- **Off (internal):** only supervisors and the assigned worker.
- **On (shared):** the property owner can read it too.

Workers have the same switch. Check it before sending anything about the
owner, pricing or other sensitive matters. Comments written by owners are
always shared.

## Properties

**Properties** → **Add property**: name, address, the owner (optional) and
**access notes**. Access notes are what workers see first at the door, so
include the key-box code, parking and wifi.

Each property can have a **cleaning checklist template**. Mark items as
*photo required* where you need proof, e.g. "Bathroom cleaned".

## Team and invites

**Team** lists supervisors, workers and owners.

- **Invite someone:** enter their email and role (and skills, for workers),
  then copy the generated link and send it to them yourself. Domar doesn't
  send emails yet. Links expire after 7 days and work once.
- **Deactivate** someone who has left. They can no longer sign in, and their
  history stays intact. **Reactivate** reverses this.
- To reset someone's password, deactivate them and send a fresh invite.
